## Combat Skill

The Combat skill is essential for increasing your efficiency against hostile mobs and unlocking advanced game content. This guide outlines the true benefits of Combat leveling in Skyblock.

> ⚠️ This guide is based on the official Hypixel Skyblock Wiki and may slightly differ from Fakepixel’s implementation.

---

📈 Combat Skill Overview

Leveling the Combat skill provides permanent bonuses:

Critical Chance <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/9/9e/SkyBlock_icons_crit_chance.png" width="18" /> (+0.5% per level)[^6]  
Coin Drops <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/3/31/SkyBlock_items_coin_iron.png" width="18" /> (increased from mobs)[^1]

---

Common XP Sources:

Slayer Quests <img src="https://cdn3.emoji.gg/emojis/3922-iron-sword.png" width="18" /> (see Slayers Guide)  
Bestiary <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/0/00/SkyBlock_icons_bestiary.png" width="18" /> progression[^3]  
Mob Grinding <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/d/dc/SkyBlock_icons_strength.png" width="18" /> (Crypt Ghouls[^7], Zealots[^8], Endermen[^9], Blazes[^15])

---

🏰 Dungeons & Slayers (Parallel XP)

Slayer Quests <img src="https://cdn3.emoji.gg/emojis/3922-iron-sword.png" width="18" />: Reliable source of Combat XP and Slayer XP. All major slayers (Revenant, Tarantula, Sven, Enderman, Blaze) are present in Skyblock; see Slayers Guide.

Dungeons <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/6/66/SkyBlock_items_dungeoneering.png" width="18" />: Unlocked at Combat 15. Provides Combat XP, Class XP, and loot.

---

## ⚙️ Damage System Overview

**Wiki Link**: [Damage System - Hypixel Wiki](https://wiki.hypixel.net/Damage)

The damage system in Skyblock is based on the legacy Hypixel formula and includes:

Strength <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/7/7d/SkyBlock_icons_strength.png" width="18" />: Increases base weapon damage  
Crit Damage <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/8/89/SkyBlock_icons_crit_damage.png" width="18" />: Amplifies damage on crits  
Crit Chance <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/9/9e/SkyBlock_icons_crit_chance.png" width="18" />: Increases chance to land crits  
Ferocity <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/0/01/SkyBlock_icons_ferocity.png" width="18" />: Chance to strike twice  
Bonus Attack Speed <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/f/fd/SkyBlock_icons_bonus_attack_speed.png" width="18" />: Reduces delay between hits  
Ability Damage <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/f/f8/SkyBlock_icons_ability_damage.png" width="18" />: Boosts skill and spell effects  
Combat Level <img src="https://wiki.hypixel.net/images/f/fe/Minecraft_items_stone_sword.png" width="18" />: Multiplies mob damage dealt  
Enchantments: E.g., Sharpness, First Strike  
Weapon Abilities: Right-click AoEs or projectiles
